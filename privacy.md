# X/Twitter Spam Filter - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 4/30/2025

## Overview
X/Twitter Spam Filter is a browser extension that helps users filter spam comments on X/Twitter. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection
We collect and process the following data:

### Website Content
- We process the text of tweets and comments that appear in your browser
- This is necessary to identify potential spam content
- All processing happens locally in your browser

### User Preferences
- Your OpenAI API key (stored locally in your browser)
- Your preference for showing/hiding spam buttons
- Your manual spam/not spam classifications

## Data Storage
- All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API
- No data is transmitted to our servers
- We do not have access to your stored data

## Third-Party Services
This extension uses the OpenAI API for spam detection:
- Your API key and tweet content are sent directly to OpenAI
- This is subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy
- We do not have access to or control over this data exchange

## Data Usage
We use the collected data only for:
- Identifying spam comments
- Improving spam detection accuracy
- Storing your preferences

## Data Sharing
We do not:
- Sell your data
- Share your data with third parties
- Use your data for purposes unrelated to spam detection

## Your Control
You can:
- Remove your API key at any time
- Delete all stored data by uninstalling the extension
- Choose whether to show spam/not spam buttons

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
